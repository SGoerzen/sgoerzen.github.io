# Customer Review

## Verdict

As a prospective customer, I would book the free introductory call, but I would not yet approve a €10,000–€69,000 project.

The positioning and expertise are convincing, but the page needs stronger evidence of results before it can justify a substantial purchase.

## Why I Would Contact You

- The specialization in XR learning, analytics, and evaluation is immediately clear.
- A doctorate, more than ten years of engineering experience, and research and delivery experience create credibility.
- The end-to-end offer is compelling: didactics, implementation, data, evaluation, and reporting—not merely an XR demo.
- Transparent pricing helps customers qualify themselves before making contact.
- The personal photo, professional design, named institutions, and concrete technologies build trust.
- The project check gives uncertain buyers a practical starting point.

## Why I Would Not Buy Yet

### Insufficient proof

The project page mainly describes what the applications do. At these prices, customers need case studies showing:

- The customer's original problem
- Sergej's specific role and deliverables
- Project duration and constraints
- Measurable or observable results
- Customer feedback or testimonials

One detailed case study with screenshots, outcomes, and a customer quote would be more persuasive than several descriptive project cards.

### Unclear conversion path

- The hero presents five competing actions.
- The primary next step is therefore less obvious than it should be.
- The project check recommends a €9,900 package by default without learning much about the customer's situation.

Use one primary CTA—**Book an introductory call**—and one secondary CTA—**View case studies**.

### Broad target audience and benefits

“Companies, universities, and research teams” covers very different buyers. The page does not yet demonstrate detailed understanding of each group's procurement, deployment, compliance, or stakeholder concerns.

The page also explains the delivery process more strongly than the final business value. It should emphasize outcomes such as:

- Measurable learning effectiveness
- Safer or faster onboarding
- Evidence for funding and stakeholder decisions
- Reduced training effort or cost
- A clear basis for scaling an XR pilot

### Trust and presentation concerns

- Introducing the freelancer network in the hero may raise questions about responsibility, data access, and who performs the work.
- The design is professional but visually generic and text-heavy for an XR specialist.
- Authentic XR screenshots, videos, dashboards, and report excerpts would make the expertise tangible.
- Packages do not state typical duration, exact deliverables, required customer involvement, or the next step after delivery.
- The contact form mixes German and English labels.
- The default social-sharing image references a missing `View1.png` file.

### Mobile experience

At a 390 px viewport, the header is broken: the name wraps into a narrow column and the navigation extends beyond the screen. This is a major conversion problem because mobile visitors may leave before reaching the offer.

## Recommended Priorities

1. Fix the mobile navigation and header.
2. Add two detailed, outcome-based case studies.
3. Reduce the hero to one primary CTA and one proof-oriented secondary CTA.
4. Add a trust strip near the hero with institutions, years of experience, delivered systems, and a testimonial.
5. Add duration, deliverables, customer responsibilities, and next steps to each package.
6. Include authentic XR and analytics visuals.
7. Move the network explanation lower on the page and clarify that Sergej remains accountable.
8. Fully localize the contact form and state the expected response time.
9. Add a valid social-sharing image.

## Possible Hero Direction

> **XR-Trainings, deren Wirksamkeit Sie nachweisen können.**  
> Konzeption, Entwicklung und Learning Analytics für Hochschulen, Forschungsteams und betriebliche Trainings—vom Pilot bis zum belastbaren Evaluationsbericht.

Primary CTA: **30-minütiges Erstgespräch buchen**  
Secondary CTA: **Fallstudien ansehen**

## Overall Assessment

- **Desktop:** 7/10
- **Mobile:** 3/10

The page sells competence convincingly, but it needs to sell demonstrated results more strongly.
